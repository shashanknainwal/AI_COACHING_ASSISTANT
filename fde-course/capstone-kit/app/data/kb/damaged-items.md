# Damaged, defective or wrong items

## Reporting damage
Report damage within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging. Leaking ink, cracked pen barrels and crushed paper all count as damage.

## What happens next
We send a free replacement by standard shipping as soon as the photo is reviewed, usually within one business day. You do not need to send the damaged item back unless we ask. If the item is out of stock we refund it in full instead.

## Wrong item received
If you received the wrong item, keep it until we send a prepaid return label. The correct item ships the same day the label is created.
