# Product care and ink compatibility

## Fountain pen care
Flush fountain pens with lukewarm water every few weeks, and when changing ink colours. Never use alcohol or hot water.

## Ink compatibility
Our Quillfeather bottled inks work in any fountain pen. Pigmented and shimmer inks need flushing more often. Iron gall inks are not recommended for pens with steel nibs that will sit unused.

## Paper
Our notebooks use 90 gsm paper rated for fountain pens. Very wet broad nibs may show slight ghosting but not bleed-through.
