# Returns and refunds

## Return window
Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Opened items in resaleable condition can be returned within 30 days for store credit. Nothing can be returned more than 60 days after delivery.

## Items we cannot take back
Custom-engraved pens, personalised notebooks, gift cards and opened ink bottles are final sale. Sample packs are final sale.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of the return arriving at the warehouse. Store credit is issued as an e-gift card by email the same day the return is checked in.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items. For change-of-mind returns a flat return label fee of $4.95 is deducted from the refund.
