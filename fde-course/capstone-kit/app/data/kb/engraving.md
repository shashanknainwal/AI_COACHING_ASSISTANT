# Engraving and personalisation

## What we can engrave
We engrave fountain pens, rollerballs and leather notebook covers. Up to 20 characters, letters and numbers only, in one of three fonts.

## Changes and cancellations
Engraving text can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of placing the order. After that the item is in production and cannot be changed.

## Mistakes
If the engraving does not match what you typed, we redo it free. If it matches what you typed but you made a typo, we can redo it for a $12 fee.
