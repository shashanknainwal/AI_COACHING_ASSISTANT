# Shipping options and delivery times

## Options
Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days and is free on orders over $40; otherwise it costs $5.95. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.95. We ship from one warehouse in Ohio.

## Order processing
Orders placed before 1 p.m. Eastern on a business day ship the same day. Orders with engraving take 2 extra business days before they ship.

## International
We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom only. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.
