# Accounts, passwords and privacy

## Password reset
Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. Support staff cannot see or set your password.

## Changing your email address
You can change the email address in Account settings after confirming your current password. For security, the old address receives a notice.

## Deleting your account and data
Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes, without marketing data. Email privacy@quillfeather.example to make a request.
