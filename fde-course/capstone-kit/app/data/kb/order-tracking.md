# Tracking an order

## Where to find tracking
A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Order numbers look like QF- followed by four digits. Customers can also see status on the Orders page of their account.

## Late orders
An order counts as late 3 business days after the latest estimated delivery date. For a late order, we open a carrier trace and, if the parcel is not found within 5 business days, we reship it free or refund it.

## Status meanings
Processing means not yet shipped. Shipped means the carrier has the parcel. Delivered means the carrier marked it delivered; if you cannot find it, check with neighbours and then contact us within 7 days.
