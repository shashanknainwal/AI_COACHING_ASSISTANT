# Billing, payments and invoices

## Payment methods
We accept major credit cards, PayPal and Quillfeather gift cards. We do not accept cheques or bank transfers for retail orders.

## Duplicate charges
A pending authorisation can appear twice for a few days when a payment is retried. Only one charge settles. If two charges have settled, we refund the duplicate within 2 business days.

## Invoices and VAT
An invoice PDF is attached to the shipping confirmation email. Business customers can add a company name and VAT number at checkout; we cannot change an invoice after the order ships.
