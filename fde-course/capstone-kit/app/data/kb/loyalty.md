# Inkwell Rewards loyalty programme

## Earning points
Members earn 1 point per dollar spent on merchandise, not on shipping or gift cards. Points appear 14 days after delivery, once the return window for most items has passed.

## Redeeming
100 points equal a $5 reward. Rewards can be used on any order and do not expire while the account is active. Points expire after 12 months with no purchases.

## Tiers
Members who spend over $300 in a calendar year reach Gold tier, which adds free express shipping on every order.
