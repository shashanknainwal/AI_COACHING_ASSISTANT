# Wholesale and bulk orders

## Who qualifies
Shops, schools and offices ordering at least $500 of merchandise per order can apply for a wholesale account. Approval takes up to 3 business days.

## Pricing and terms
Wholesale accounts get 25 percent off list price and can pay by invoice with 30-day terms after two prepaid orders.

## Bulk engraving
Bulk engraving of more than 25 items adds 5 business days to processing.
