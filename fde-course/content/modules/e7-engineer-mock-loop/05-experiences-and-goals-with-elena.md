## How this round works

On the right, **Elena Sorokin** runs the conversation for about eight turns. Elena is a fictional practice interviewer played by Claude, not a real person at any lab.

1. Answer each question in under two minutes, with one concrete example.
2. Use your own stories. Module C5's story bank is the right source; don't write new stories the night before.
3. Expect one gentle push for something you'd do differently. Have a real answer.
4. After six to eight answers, press **End and get feedback** for a scored debrief.

## What each area is really asking

| Area | The question under the question |
|---|---|
| Motivation | Is this role a deliberate choice, or one of fifty applications? |
| Customer work | Will you find the real problem when the customer describes a different one? |
| Conflict | Can you disagree well, and describe the other side fairly? |
| Career goals | Will this role still fit you in two years? |
| Self-awareness | Do you know your weak spots, and are you working on them? |

## Prepare in ten minutes

Pick one story from your bank for each of the customer, conflict and "do differently" areas, and write one line for each: the situation, what you did, the outcome. Then write one sentence on why applied AI, in words only you could have written. Use the same honesty rules as every round: real experience only, and no AI help during the conversation itself.
