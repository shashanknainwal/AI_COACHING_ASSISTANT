The FDE track covers scope control in "Scope, Change Requests, and the Art of Saying No" (module 02). This lesson is the executive version: the same no, said to someone with more power than you, under time pressure.

## A shape for saying no

1. **The answer first.** "No. A full launch on December 1st doesn't meet the bar we agreed, and I don't recommend it."
2. **Why, in their terms.** The number, then what it means for a real policyholder.
3. **What we can do.** A specific alternative with a date. Something she can still announce.
4. **What it takes.** Dependencies, owners, the next checkpoint.
5. **Help with the message.** One sentence she can say to the board.

Never let "no" be the last thing you say. The last thing you say should be the plan.

## How this practice works

Joanna opens. Answer her question honestly. She'll push back several times. After at least four exchanges, press **End and get feedback**.

## Before you start

- Decide what you'll offer for December 1st. Look at which categories already pass the bar.
- Decide what you'll say if she asks for a disclaimer instead of a fix.
- Write the one sentence you'd want her to say to the board.
